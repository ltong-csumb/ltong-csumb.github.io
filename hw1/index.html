Overview of SDLC

A structured approach to software development goes through different stages, starting with planning and ending with a plan for ongoing maintenance.

How Does SDLC work?

There are seven phases: Planning, Analysis, Design, Implementation, Testing, Deployment, Maintenance.

Benefits of SDLC

  • Better communication and collaboration
  • Improved organization and efficiency
  • Consistence and higher quality software.
  • Reduced security risks and help lower cost
